# Project facts and sequence

The console combines phone charging, accessible power sockets, a clock, storage and illuminated trim in a removable panel around the gear lever.

## Why I built it

The factory centre console in my Toyota Wildlander sat too low for my hand, left little useful phone space and had a charging adapter that stuck out. I wanted a raised panel with charging within reach and lights along its edges. I measured the existing console to make the panel fit over it.

## Sequence

- I began the 3D design in November 2025.
- I planned the circuit before cutting and assembling the panel.
- I started building the console in January 2026, cutting and covering the boards, fitting the components and wiring the panel.
- The photographs show the completed build. The tape photographs and table scan were taken in October 2026.
- The saved parametric model and circuit drawings are later technical documentation of the completed panel. 

## Components and behaviour

- I measured the existing centre console and sized the removable panel to sit over it, leaving clearance for the components underneath.
- The silver button latches in the depressed position and keeps the RGB lights on after release. Its operation is confirmed; the hidden terminal routing and which conductor it interrupts are not established in the drawing.
- The red-ringed capped fitting is a 12 V accessory socket. The adjacent USB socket has a blue illuminated ring.
- Clear light fibres run along the middle of the top bands of the two side walls. LED heads under the rear corners feed these fibres. A third LED head lights the gear-lever area.
- The RGB controller is on the outside bottom of the cup body. It is not mounted inside the cup.
- The clock sits near the back of the gear-lever notch and runs on its own battery, independently of the console supply.
- The recorded light sequence is blue → red → green, with abrupt changes approximately every 0.55 seconds.
- The Qi pad sits separately in the tray ahead of the gear lever and connects through a two-pin lead to the console's 12 V supply. The installed drawing marks its concealed return route with a dashed line.
