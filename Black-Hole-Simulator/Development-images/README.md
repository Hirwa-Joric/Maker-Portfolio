# Rendering stills

Stills from the 29 October 2026 rendering session, at 1920 × 1080 on screen. They show the renderer's resolution settings, not earlier versions or the exact state of later builds. The date in each filename identifies the rendering session.

- [Coarse view](Coarse-view-2026-10-29.png): the image traced at 160 × 90, as a fast-moving preview can look.
- [Close view](Close-view-2026-10-29.png): 8 Schwarzschild radii from the hole at 1280 × 720, with the lensed sky grid.
- [Resolution comparison](Resolution-comparison-2026-10-29.png): the centre of the same view at 160 × 90 (left) and 1600 × 900 (right).
- [Preview](Preview-2026-10-29.png): the disk, suns and starfield at 1600 × 900.

The [build guide](../sim/README.md) explains the resolution and recording options.
