# Shape and component checks

These checks compare my finished console with its explanatory model, using photographs, tape measurements and the October scan. They record the panel's outline, component positions and visible construction.

| Feature | Current representation | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Side walls | Pointed tips on the top edge; lower edge beneath the deck | Side views, tape and scan |
| Deck and notch | Tapered deck, widening notch, rounded arm noses | Top photographs and scan |
| Bands | Fibres near the middle of the top face | IMG_3449, IMG_3465, IMG_3468, IMG_3490, IMG_3501 |
| Clock | Low body with black outline, chrome bezel and glass | Tape and close views |
| Button and socket | Silver button latches down to keep the lights on; red ring surrounds a 12 V socket | My close-ups and operation of the latching button |
| Underside | Felt, bar, sockets, clips and controller on outer cup bottom | IMG_3477–3482 and IMG_3502–3504 |
| Flexible bands | Fitted and bowed table poses | IMG_3483, IMG_3488, IMG_3491 |
| Light cycle | Blue → red → green, approximately 0.55 s each | Recorded demonstration |

[Photo and model comparisons](../3d/renders/compare/) place the finished panel beside renders from fitted cameras. Current values are in [Geometry reference](GEOMETRY.md) and [params.scad](../3d/scad/params.scad). Hidden padding, cavities, wires and cabin surroundings are simplified. A photographic match does not measure unseen geometry or test a circuit. The scan does not cover the underside.
