# Maker Portfolio

I'm Joric. This repository holds two things I made: a program that calculates what a black hole looks like, and a console I built for my car. In both, I check the result against something real: the simulator's shadow against Schwarzschild's exact prediction, and the console's model against photographs, tape measurements and a 3D scan of the finished panel.

I developed the code locally and recently uploaded this finished snapshot to GitHub.

| Project | What it is | Start here |
|---|---|---|
| [Black-Hole-Simulator](Black-Hole-Simulator/) | An interactive C++/OpenGL simulator of Sagittarius A*. It traces light through curved spacetime on the GPU; suns pull on one another and bend starlight, and a sun is torn apart by the hole's tides; the shadow matches the prediction within 0.4%. | [Overview](Black-Hole-Simulator/README.md) · [Build guide](Black-Hole-Simulator/sim/README.md) · [Validation](Black-Hole-Simulator/sim/VALIDATION.md) |
| [Car-Console](Car-Console/) | A removable leatherette console for my Toyota Wildlander, with phone space, USB and 12 V sockets, a clock and side-glow light. A separate wireless pad sits on the front tray and draws power from the console's 12 V supply. | [Overview](Car-Console/README.md) · [Wiring](Car-Console/circuit/README.md) · [3D model](Car-Console/3d/README.md) |
